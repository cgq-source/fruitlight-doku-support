Privacy policy

About this policy

This policy describes information handled by Guiquan Chen (cgq-source), developer of Fruitlight Doku, in the app, support correspondence and this website. Contact: abc466254511@gmail.com.

01 · Game records stay on your device

The app locally stores campaign and daily progress, boards and marks, undo history, hearts, revival status, light seeds, hint receipts, claimed rewards, and language, music, sound, haptic and reduced-motion settings. They let you resume play and avoid duplicate charges or rewards. They are not uploaded to the developer.

02 · Current app features

The app has no developer-operated account, advertising SDK, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking. Pro is a one-time purchase provided through Apple StoreKit. Apple handles payment; the app verifies transactions and purchase entitlements. The developer receives no payment-card details and operates no transaction-collection server. If you open the optional Game Center leaderboard and sign in, the app submits your number of completed campaign levels to Apple Game Center and uses the Game Center player identifier for ranking. Apple displays player aliases and scores according to its settings. You can play without signing in. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Apple store, purchase and Game Center services follow Apple’s privacy policy.

03 · Email support

If you email us, we receive your address, message and voluntary attachments to reply and investigate. Do not send passwords, payment details or unrelated personal information. Email providers process messages for delivery and storage. Correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve requests or meet applicable obligations.

04 · Storage, deletion and backups

Game records remain locally until you or the system remove them. Deleting the app may remove progress, light seeds and hint records. System backups depend on device settings. Versions with iCloud sync can restore a previously synced save; unsynced or deleted data cannot be guaranteed recoverable. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence we hold.

05 · This website

This website uses GitHub Pages. It adds no advertising, analytics, tracking cookies or forms, and uses no external image or font CDN. GitHub may process technical requests such as IP addresses and browser information to serve and protect the site. Visiting policy pages does not upload game saves.

06 · Processing and your rights

Support correspondence is processed to answer your request, for our legitimate interest in providing support, or to meet applicable legal obligations. GitHub, Apple and email providers may process information abroad under their policies. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent where relied upon, and complaint to a regulator. Contact us about information we hold; providers handle their own processing.

07 · Children and updates

The app does not ask players for names, ages, contact details or location. Guardians may contact us about information a child voluntarily included in support correspondence. We will update this page and its effective date when our information practices change.

08 · Optional iCloud sync

In versions that support this feature, sync starts only after you enable it. Game progress, boards, light-seed history, claimed rewards and unlock records are saved in your CloudKit private database for devices using the same iCloud account. A container-specific account identifier prevents automatic transfers after account changes; the app does not obtain your iCloud password or email. Saves use your iCloud storage and are not sent to a developer-operated server. Language and sound preferences remain device-specific. Turning sync off deletes neither save. You can manage this app’s cloud data in Apple’s iCloud storage settings. Before resolving a conflict, both saves are backed up locally; removing the app may remove these backups. Apple processes iCloud services under its privacy policy.

Purchase wallet data

Wallet-enabled versions store balances, product IDs, Apple transaction IDs, transaction environments, credit and spending records, reward receipts and unlocks in your private CloudKit database. A random wallet ID is sent to Apple as appAccountToken to associate transactions; a container-specific account ID helps prevent accidental transfers between iCloud accounts. The developer receives no card details or iCloud credentials and operates no wallet server. Wallet storage is separate from progress sync; switching progress sync off does not clear it. Unsynced balance changes and gameplay actions exist only on the originating device and may be lost if the app is deleted.
