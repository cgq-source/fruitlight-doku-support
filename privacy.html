Purchase wallet data

Wallet-enabled versions store balances, product IDs, Apple transaction IDs, transaction environments, credit and spending records, reward receipts and unlocks in your private CloudKit database. A random wallet ID is sent to Apple as appAccountToken to associate transactions; a container-specific account ID helps prevent accidental transfers between iCloud accounts. The developer receives no card details or iCloud credentials and operates no wallet server. Wallet storage is separate from progress sync; switching progress sync off does not clear it. Unsynced balance changes and gameplay actions exist only on the originating device and may be lost if the app is deleted.

Timed Challenge records and Game Center

Timed Challenge locally stores the active board and timer state, random run identifier, recent board records, best scores, and the date and run identifiers used for free attempts. Guest scores use a random guest identifier. When signed in to Game Center, the app uses the game-scoped player identifier to distinguish scores and submits the best Timed Challenge score to Apple Game Center; the campaign leaderboard receives the number of completed levels. Apple displays player aliases and scores according to its settings. With iCloud progress sync enabled, best scores, recent boards and free-attempt records are included in saves in your private CloudKit database. An active timed run remains only on its original device and cannot be resumed on another device. The developer does not collect these records on a developer-operated server. Core gameplay does not require Game Center sign-in.

Orchard Expedition saves

Supported versions store completed stops, the current stop, lantern positions, move counts and undo history on this device for offline play and replay. Expedition saves are excluded from iCloud progress sync and are not submitted to Game Center or a developer-operated server. App removal, device loss or data cleanup may erase them. Restore Purchases cannot recover expedition progress on another device.

Rating reminder records

The app stores the first-play time, play dates, rating-request dates and versions, and the version in which the rating link was opened only on this device to reduce repeated prompts. These records are excluded from the iCloud wallet and progress sync and are not uploaded to the developer. Apple StoreKit provides the rating prompt and the system decides whether to show it. Apple handles ratings and reviews you submit to the App Store; the app does not read your star rating or review text.

App update queries

Supported versions may check store information on Home, and you can check manually in Settings. Over HTTPS, the app sends its App Store ID and store country or region code to Apple's iTunes lookup service to read the public version, release notes and minimum system requirement. It does not send saves, wallet balances or player identifiers. The store region comes from StoreKit, with the device region setting as a fallback. Check times, returned store information and snooze records stay on this device and are excluded from iCloud sync. Apple may process technical request information such as IP addresses under its privacy policy. The developer operates no update-query server.
