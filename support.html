Support

Email abc466254511@gmail.com for gameplay or privacy questions. You may include app and iOS versions and a description. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Progress and dew

The game saves locally, with separate campaign and daily boards. Replays do not repeat first-clear rewards; undo is free. Removing the app may erase progress. There are no accounts, cloud sync or purchase restoration in this version.

Tutorial, sound and daily puzzles

The illustrated tutorial in Settings explains lamps, numbered walls and dual-light buds. Music, effects, haptics and reduced motion have separate settings. Daily puzzles change on the UTC date, which may differ from local midnight.
